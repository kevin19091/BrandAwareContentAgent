Plum Goodness (founded 2013) is India's first 100% vegan beauty brand,
PETA-certified cruelty-free, formulated around "good science" — natural
nutrients plus research-backed actives, in recyclable packaging. Its ethos
is "goodness that delivers": ethics (vegan, cruelty-free) as the primary
purchase trigger, backed by science-first credibility rather than pure
ingredient storytelling.

Technique gap vs. Mamaearth: Plum leads with ethical-identity positioning
(vegan, cruelty-free as a stance); Mamaearth leads with parental-safety
positioning (toxin-free as reassurance for a health-conscious family).
Mamaearth-adjacent copy should keep the safety/reassurance framing distinct
from Plum's ethics-forward vegan identity.
