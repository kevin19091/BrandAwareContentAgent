BlueStone (founded Bangalore, 2011) is India's digital-first fine-jewellery
brand, built around lifestyle jewellery bought for work, gifting, and
self-expression rather than the traditional wedding/investment-led purchase
occasion. It leans hard into modern, digital-native positioning — 360-degree
campaigns like "Big Gold Upgrade" and #ThreesToOne run across performance
marketing, influencer content, and BTL, aimed at a fashion-and-aspiration
buyer rather than a heritage buyer.

Technique gap vs. Fabindia: BlueStone sells modernity and digital
convenience as the aspiration; Fabindia sells rootedness and artisan
authenticity as the aspiration. Fabindia-adjacent copy can borrow BlueStone's
occasion-diversity idea (product for everyday self-expression, not just
festive/heritage occasions) without borrowing its digital-modernity voice.
