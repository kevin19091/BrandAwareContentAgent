Source: The North Face brand strategy coverage; IIDE, BrandVM, Modern Retail,
Hype Insight, 2026.

The North Face repositioned in 2024-2026 around three core categories (snow,
climb, trail) after chasing adjacent categories too broadly, and wants to be
seen as a "democratic" brand — quality technical gear for everyday use, not
just elite athletes. Its SS26 "Never Just a Hike" campaign frames every
outdoor trip as carrying a personal story, shifting emphasis from gear
specs to the meaning of the journey, backed by 200+ sponsored athletes
including Alex Honnold.

Technique gap vs. Patagonia: The North Face's story is about the individual
experience of the outdoors (narrative, personal meaning); Patagonia's is
about the planet the outdoors depends on (activism, environmental urgency).
Patagonia-adjacent copy should keep the planet-first framing distinct from
The North Face's personal-journey narrative style.
