Source: Patagonia mission/values pages via BusinessModelAnalyst, FourWeekMBA,
Institute of Sustainability Studies, Purpose Economy, 2026 analyses.

Mission: "We're in business to save our home planet" (in place since 2018,
replacing earlier "build the best product" framing).

Core values (refreshed 2022, 50th anniversary): Quality (absorbs the "build
the best product" heritage) and Environmentalism (carries the "protect our
home planet" thread) sit alongside a broader identity as environmental
activist and anti-consumerist — the brand positions itself as more than a
retailer, closer to a movement.

Voice: plainspoken, values-first, allergic to hype. Openly discusses its own
environmental impact and corporate practices rather than only promoting
product — this transparency is itself a trust-building device, not a
disclaimer bolted on.

Marketing approach: Worn Wear (repair/resale instead of new purchases) and
activism-driven campaigns over conventional promotional pushes; growth is
expected to happen without compromising the mission. In 2022 the Chouinard
family transferred ownership to a trust and nonprofit specifically to protect
this independence.

Pillars: environmental urgency, quality/durability over disposability,
radical transparency, activism over advertising.
