Adidas' primary multi-year platform "You Got This" (launched Feb 2024,
running through 2026) takes a softer, more supportive angle than Nike's
individual-potential framing — it addresses the pressure that drains joy from
sport, and reframes support networks (parents, coaches, friends) as part of an
athlete's success rather than centering the lone athlete against the odds.
Chapter two, "We All Need Someone to Make Us Believe," leans into a
"Sideline Effect" sub-theme: the people cheering you on matter as much as you
do.

Technique gap vs. Nike: Adidas' softer, communal-support tone is a
differentiator worth noting when writing Nike-adjacent copy — Nike's brief
should stay individual-facing ("what if you don't lose") rather than drifting
into Adidas' communal-support territory, to keep messaging distinct from the
category's other major player.
