Source: Nike "Why Do It?" campaign (2026 relaunch of "Just Do It"), CMO Nicole Graham
public remarks; MarketingDive, RetailDive, WWD coverage, July 2026.

Voice: bold, motivational, unflinching. Speaks to the athlete in everyone, not
just elite performers. Comfortable sitting with doubt and pressure rather than
papering over it — the 2026 "Why Do It?" reframing explicitly leans into
sociopolitical and economic pressure young athletes feel, then answers it with
belief and forward motion rather than denial.

Core message: effort and the decision to begin matter more than the outcome.
Tyler, the Creator's narration frames it directly: "You could give everything
you have and still lose... but what if you don't?" The brand's chief marketing
officer describes "Just Do It" as "a spirit that lives in every heartbeat of
sport... the belief that, together, we can inspire, unite and elevate
ourselves beyond what we thought possible."

Visual style: slow-motion hero footage of athletes at high-stakes moments,
global sports stars (Alcaraz, Barkley, Clark, LeBron, Vini Jr.) used as
proof-points, cinematic and epic in tone, distributed across live sports,
social, streaming, cinema, and out-of-home.

Pillars: courage, self-belief, potential, unity through sport.
